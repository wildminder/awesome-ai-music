# LeVo 2 (SongGeneration 2)

Open-source foundation model for commercial-grade music generation by Tencent AI Lab. It outperforms open-source baselines and rivals commercial systems in Overall Quality, Melody, Arrangement, Sound Quality, and Structure.

## Links

- GitHub: https://github.com/tencent-ailab/songgeneration
- HuggingFace: https://huggingface.co/tencent/SongGeneration
- Demo: https://huggingface.co/spaces/tencent/SongGeneration

## Features

- architecture: Hybrid LLM-Diffusion
- music_gen: yes
- input_modalities: text (lyrics, multilingual), audio prompt
- streaming: no
- languages: Zh, En
- license: Apache-2.0
- lyrics_support: yes (Chinese, English)
- multilingual: yes (Zh, En)
- text_audio_prompts: yes
- text_to_music: yes
- vram: 12GB-22GB

## Innovation

Hybrid LLM-Diffusion approach that combines autoregressive language modeling with diffusion-based audio synthesis, enabling commercial-grade full-song generation with both Chinese and English lyrics.
